About Aethoshi

Good tools, without the complicated software.

Aethoshi is a one-person studio building practical apps for small business owners. They need good tools but have no time to learn complicated software.

Aethoshi

The name comes from aether, the old Greek word for the clear, bright air above the clouds. That's how we want our apps to feel: light and clear, and out of your way, so the work gets done and you get back to your customers. The logo is a star on its orbit: one small, steady light in that clear sky.

What we believe

Four promises in every app.

  • Simple first

    If a feature needs a manual, it isn't finished. Every screen should make sense on first use.

  • Your data is yours

    Apps work offline and keep your information on your own phone. No accounts, and nothing uploaded to us.

  • Fair, not free-for-all

    Free to use with a few ads, kept away from the screens where you work.

  • Built to last

    Backups you control and careful testing, so years of records stay safe.
