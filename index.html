About Aethoshi

Aethoshi is a one-person studio building practical apps for people who run small businesses: the clinic down the road, the corner shop, the neighbourhood salon. These are people who need good tools but have no time for complicated software.

The name

Aethoshi comes from aether, the old Greek word for the clear, bright air above the clouds. That's how we want our apps to feel: light and clear, and out of your way, so the work gets done and you get back to your customers.

What we believe

  • Simple firstIf a feature needs a manual, it isn't finished. Every screen should make sense on first use.
  • Your data is yoursApps work offline and keep your information on your own phone. No accounts, and nothing uploaded to us.
  • Fair, not free-for-allOur apps are free with a few ads, kept away from the screens where you work.
  • Built to lastBackups you control and careful testing, so years of records are safe.
