Parchi privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026

This policy explains how Parchi (the "app"), made by Aethoshi ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: your business data stays on your phone. We don't collect it, and we can't see it. The only data that leaves your phone is what our advertising partner, Google AdMob, collects to show ads, and anything you choose to send yourself.

1. Data you enter stays on your device

Everything you put into Parchi is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. That covers your business details, customers, patients or clients, items, invoices, payments, appointments, expenses, logos, signatures and receipt photos. Parchi has no account, no login and no server of ours. We do not receive, store or sell this data.

2. Things you choose to share

Some features send data out of the app, but only when you tap them:

  • Sharing or printing an invoice. The PDF goes to the app or printer you pick (for example WhatsApp, email or a printer).
  • Backups and exports. A backup file or Excel / CSV export is created on your phone and saved or sent wherever you choose (for example Google Drive, email or your computer). Those copies are then handled by the service you chose, under its own privacy policy.
  • Call and WhatsApp buttons. These open your phone's dialer or WhatsApp with the number filled in. Parchi itself does not make the call or send the message.
  • Problem reports. If you choose "Report a problem", or "Send report" after a crash, your email app opens with a message to us. Attached are the app's technical log, the app version, your phone model and its Android version. The log records events and errors only. It is written so that it does not contain your customers' names, phone numbers, amounts or other business data. You can read the email before you send it, or not send it at all. We use reports only to fix bugs, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Parchi is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect, for example:

DataWhy
Advertising ID and other device identifiers Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location (from your IP address) Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Ad interactions (views, taps) Advertising, analytics
Crash logs and diagnostics of the ads SDK Analytics, fraud prevention

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. It is encrypted in transit. Google's use is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Ads never use the business data from section 1.

Your choices:

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Parchi asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in More → Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the wording varies by phone).

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state. To load ads. Billing works fully offline.
  • Biometrics (fingerprint or face). Only if you turn on App lock. Android checks your fingerprint or face. Parchi never receives or stores it.
  • Camera and photos. Only when you choose to add a logo, signature or receipt photo. The picture is saved in Parchi's own storage.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete records in the app; deleted invoices and expenses wait in the Recycle bin for 30 days first. Uninstalling Parchi, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently deletes everything it stored on the phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete. Because we hold none of your business data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To delete a problem report you sent, email us.

6. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can add App lock for extra protection. Please keep backups, because data on a lost or broken phone can't be recovered without one.

7. Children

Parchi is a business tool for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email aethoshi.studio@gmail.com.
