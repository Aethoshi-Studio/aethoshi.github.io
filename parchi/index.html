Parchi

Billing made simple for clinics, shops and salons.

Android · Coming soon to Google Play

Parchi is for small businesses that want clean, correct bills without learning accounting software. Open it, add what you sold, and share a professional invoice in seconds.

What you can do

  • Invoices in secondsPick a customer and items, and share a PDF on WhatsApp or print it.
  • GST done rightCGST and SGST, or IGST for another state, with place of supply, HSN/SAC and Bill of Supply.
  • Payments and duesRecord cash, UPI or card payments and see who still owes you.
  • AppointmentsBook visits, mark them done or no-show, and bill them in one tap.
  • Clients and itemsSaved customers with their own IDs, and a price list you can reuse.
  • Expenses and reportsIncome, expenses and profit by month, with Excel / CSV export.
  • BranchesSeveral outlets that share clients and items, each with its own invoices.
  • Backups you controlSave a backup file to Drive, email or a computer, and restore it any time.

Your data stays on your phone

Parchi works without an account and without the internet. Your invoices, customers and reports are stored only on your device. We never see them. Read the full privacy policy.

Free, with ads

Parchi is free to use. It shows a small banner on screens you read, never on forms. Now and then, after you share, print or save a bill, a full-screen ad may appear: at most once every 10 minutes, and never in your first 3 days.

Help and contact

The app has a built-in Help & guide (More → Help & guide). For anything else, email aethoshi.studio@gmail.com.
