Features

Everything a small business bills with.

From the first invoice to the end-of-month report, all in one app on your phone.

  • Invoices in seconds

    Pick a customer and items, then share a PDF on WhatsApp or print it.

  • GST done right

    CGST and SGST, or IGST for another state, with place of supply, HSN/SAC and Bill of Supply.

  • Payments and dues

    Record cash, UPI or card payments and see who still owes you.

  • Appointments

    Book visits, mark them done or no-show, and bill them in one tap.

  • Clients and items

    Saved customers with their own IDs, and a price list you can reuse.

  • Expenses and reports

    Income, expenses and profit by month, with Excel / CSV export.

  • Branches

    Several outlets that share clients and items, each with its own invoices.

  • Backups you control

    Save a backup to Drive, email or a computer, and restore it any time.

Your data stays on your phone.

No account and no internet needed to bill. Your invoices, customers and reports are stored only on your device, and we never see them. Read the privacy policy.

Free, with ads

Ads that stay out of your way.

Parchi is free to use. It shows a small banner on screens you read, never on forms. Now and then, after you share, print or save a bill, a full-screen ad may appear: at most once every 10 minutes, and never in your first 3 days.
